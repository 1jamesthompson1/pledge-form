{schoolName} collect personal information from you and use it to process this pledge, to enrol and care for your children, and to meet our legal duties. This statement explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and your rights. Last updated: {year}.

What we collect. Parent and guardian names, the other parent or guardian's name, email address, children's names, class or age and days of attendance, emergency contacts, medical and health information, custodial and financial arrangements, your consent choices for EOTC, health, conduct and photos, the amounts you pledge, and your signature and date.

Why we collect it. To record and manage your pledge and donation and issue receipts; to keep your children safe and provide for their health and learning; to obtain and record the consents required for EOTC, medical care and photos; to communicate with you; and to meet our legal and record-keeping obligations.

Who we share it with. We share it with a small number of school staff who process pledge amounts with Kindo and manage various consent processes. If required due to more complex situations, a wider group of staff may be involved and see the information. We may also disclose information where the law requires or permits it.

Storage and overseas processing. Information is stored securely and access is restricted. Some of our providers store or process data outside New Zealand (for example, Microsoft Azure data centres in Australia). We use contracts and security controls to require them to protect your information.

Voluntary or required. Giving us this information is voluntary, but if you do not provide it, we may not be able to process your pledge, record your consents, or keep your child safe and properly cared for.

Access and correction. You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. For privacy questions, contact {trustAdminContact}.

How long we keep it. We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above and to meet our record-keeping requirements. Pledge submissions and their audit records are kept for two years from the date they are submitted, and are then securely deleted.

Photos. You can change or withdraw your photo consent at any time by contacting the school office.

Complaints. If you have a privacy concern, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz or 0800 803 909.
